About WWPB

Makayla Wright

Hi, I'm Makayla, the owner and creator of Wright Way Photo Booths 🐝

I started Wright Way Photo Booths with one simple goal: to create a fun, welcoming experience that brings people together and adds something special to every event. I love helping guests step out of the crowd, have fun in front of the camera, and walk away with photos they're excited to keep.

At Wright Way Photo Booths, I offer an interactive photo booth experience designed to get guests laughing, engaging, and creating memories from the very first snap. Whether it's a wedding, corporate event, or celebration with family and friends, my booth becomes a space where people connect, let loose, and enjoy the moment together.

My name says it all — I do things the Wright Way. That means showing up prepared, professional, and genuinely excited to be part of your event. From setup to breakdown, I focus on making the entire experience smooth, stress-free, and enjoyable for both you and your guests.

Inspired by the idea of spreading positivity the way bees spread pollen, Wright Way Photo Booths is about more than photos — it's about creating moments of joy that your guests will remember long after the event ends. I'm so grateful to be part of your celebration and can't wait to help capture memories you'll love looking back on.
